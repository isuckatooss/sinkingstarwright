# <img width="48" height="48" alt="" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/204f8081-0581-4ae9-8218-ecb5c6cbb13b" align="absmiddle" /> Sinking StarWright

Create, play and share custom levels for [Order of the Sinking Star](https://www.orderofthesinkingstar.com).

**Download the app at [sinkingstarwright.com](https://sinkingstarwright.com)** (Windows).

This repository is for **bug reports and feature requests** only. The app's source code isn't
hosted here.

## Reporting a bug

1. [Search the existing issues](../../issues?q=is%3Aissue) first. If yours is there, add a 👍 or a
   comment with anything new.
2. Otherwise, [open a bug report](../../issues/new?template=bug_report.yml) and tell me:
   - **What happened, and what you expected.** Screenshots or a short video help a lot.
   - **The steps to make it happen again.**
   - **The app's version.** Click your profile (or **Guest**) at the bottom left of the home
     screen. The version is at the bottom of that menu.
   - **The level, if it's about one.** On home, open the level's **⋮** menu, choose **Export to file**,
     and attach the exported JSON file. You can also choose **Copy JSON to clipboard**, then paste it
     into the issue (or into [Pastebin](https://pastebin.com) and link it).

If something went wrong with your game save, don't panic: the app backs up your save every time
it starts the game. Open the menu at the bottom left and choose **Restore backup**.

## Requesting a feature

[Open a feature request](../../issues/new?template=feature_request.yml) and describe what you'd
like to do and why.

## Privacy

The app counts downloads, launches and how many levels are made, anonymously. You can opt out in
the app: menu at the bottom left, **Privacy notice**.
